The challenge

Long-term investments are decided under deep uncertainty

  • Energy infrastructure and supply chains tie up significant capital, for a long time.
  • The interactions between capacity, flows and demand go beyond what intuition and spreadsheets can capture.
  • A sound choice made in isolation can degrade the performance of the whole system.

The solution

The optimisation digital twin

Your system, its interactions and its constraints become a reusable model that computes the optimal decisions to take.

What the solution delivers

A tool built to be used over time, not a static report.

  • Modelled as a graph of nodes

    The system is represented by its components and the links between them: facilities, flows, constraints.

  • Easy to use, change and improve

    The model evolves with your system, without starting from scratch.

  • Multi-objective optimisation

    Minimise costsMaximise diversity of supply

  • Robust and stochastic studies

    Decisions that hold up under uncertainty.

  • Sensitivity analyses

    Identify the parameters that really drive the outcome.

  • Extension to new scenarios

    The same twin answers the next questions too.

When things change, the twin helps you

Understand

What does the change mean for current operations?

Correct

How can operations be adapted, quickly and over the long term?

Anticipate

Assess the robustness of the new system against future changes.

Why a digital twin

Where conventional approaches fall short

External studies, expert opinion, one-off models: each answers one question, once. The twin answers the next one too.

Conventional approach Its limitation With the digital twin
External studies A consultancy assesses the changes to make. One specific report per scenario, with little or no adaptability. A reusable model: every new scenario is computed on the same twin.
Domain expertise Decisions rest on expert opinion. It is hard to quantify whether you could do better. Optimal decisions are computed and quantified, under the system's real constraints.
Single-use models A model built for a single question, hard to maintain and to interact with. A tool that is easy to use, change and extend, kept alive by your own teams.
